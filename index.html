Anika Roy

Painting is the way to narrate my stories and my imagination. From childhood, working with brush and color has been my central artistic pursuit.

Spanning over 15 years of creative output, my practice balances fine art, Rickshaw painting motifs, detailed pen sketchbook studies, illustration narratives, and large-scale decorative sets.
