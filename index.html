About the Artist

Painting is the way to narrate my stories and imagination. Since childhood, working with brush and color has been my primary form of expression.

Spanning traditional oil on canvas, introspective acrylic series, pen sketchbooks, and heritage rickshaw art, this collection marks a reflective exploration across 15 years of creative pursuit.
